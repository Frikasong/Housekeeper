ℹ️ What is an Evidence Brief? ▼
An Evidence Brief is a formal PDF document you submit to the Landlord and Tenant Board before your hearing. It organizes your supporting documents into numbered tabs, making it easy for the adjudicator to find evidence as you refer to it during the hearing.

Typically, each side submits their own brief. Label your tabs clearly — during the hearing, you will refer to your evidence by page number: "I refer you to page 5 of my Evidence Brief, the lease agreement." Your generated PDF includes a Table of Contents so the adjudicator can quickly turn to the right page.

ℹ️ Tips for strong evidence ▼
  • Photos: Clear, well-lit, and date-stamped if possible — show the specific issue
  • Documents: Include the full document, not just a highlighted page
  • Communications: Screenshot or export the full thread, not just one message
  • File formats: Images (JPG/PNG/HEIC), PDFs, Word docs, and most common formats are all supported
  • Sensitive info: Need to hide personal details before submitting? In Step 4 (Review & Generate), hover any file and click the ✂ Redact button — then click and drag to draw black boxes over anything you want removed (e.g. SIN, bank account numbers, signatures). Changes are applied permanently to that file before the PDF is generated.

📋 Attending your LTB Hearing — what to expect ▼
  • Submit your Evidence Brief through the Tribunals Ontario Portal before your hearing deadline — your submission is digital, so everything is handled online
  • Serve the opposing party — deliver a copy of your Evidence Brief to the other party before the hearing (check your Notice of Hearing for the required deadline)
  • Join your hearing via Zoom — LTB hearings are conducted online; check your Notice of Hearing for the Zoom link and scheduled time
  • Join 10–15 minutes early to test your audio and video before the hearing starts
  • Refer to your evidence by page number during the hearing: "I refer you to page 5 of my Evidence Brief, the lease agreement" — the Table of Contents in your PDF lists each tab's starting page
  • Listen carefully and give the adjudicator time to finish before responding

This tool helps you organize documents for your hearing. It is not legal advice. If available, consider speaking with a tenant duty counsel before your hearing.

About Housekeeper

Housekeeper is a free, open-source web tool that helps tenants prepare professional evidence briefs for Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board (LTB) hearings.

Upload your evidence files — photos, PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, or iPhone HEIC images — organize them into numbered tabs, and generate a court-ready PDF with a title page, table of contents, tab dividers, and properly numbered pages.

Why We Built This

LTB hearings can be stressful and unfamiliar for tenants who are representing themselves. Organizing evidence in a format adjudicators expect — and knowing how to present it confidently — makes a real difference. We built Housekeeper to lower that barrier.

How It Works

  • Enter your case information and case type (N4/N5)
  • Upload your evidence files in any common format
  • Organize files into labelled tabs matching evidence categories
  • Redact any sensitive information before generating
  • Download a court-ready Evidence Brief PDF, ready to submit through the Tribunals Ontario Portal
