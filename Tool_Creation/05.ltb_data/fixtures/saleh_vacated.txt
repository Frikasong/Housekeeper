[LANDLORD] (the 'Landlord') applied for an order to terminate the tenancy and evict [Tenant 1] (the 'Tenant') because the Tenant did not pay the rent that the Tenant owes. This application was heard by videoconference on June 11, 2026. Only the Landlord attended the hearing.
As of 1:26 p.m., the Tenant was not present or represented at the hearing although properly served with notice of this hearing by the LTB. There was no record of a request to adjourn the hearing. As a result, the hearing proceeded with only the Landlord's evidence.
Determinations:
1. The Landlord served the Tenant with a valid Notice to End Tenancy Early for Non-payment of Rent (N4 Notice). The Tenant did not void the notice by paying the amount of rent arrears owing by the termination date in the N4 Notice or before the date the application was filed.
3. The Tenant vacated the rental unit on March 19, 2026. Rent arrears are calculated up to the date the Tenant vacated the unit
4. The lawful rent is $3,400.00. It was due on the 15th day of each month.
6. The rent arrears owing to March 19, 2026 are $15,358.90.
It is ordered that:
1. The tenancy between the Landlord and the Tenant is terminated as of March 19, 2026, the date the Tenant moved out of the rental unit.
