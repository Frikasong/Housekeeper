CAPREIT Limited Partnership (the 'Landlord') applied for an order to terminate the tenancy and evict [Tenant 1] (the 'Tenant') because the Tenant did not pay the rent that the Tenant owes.
This application was scheduled to be heard by videoconference on July 28, 2026. The Landlord’s Representative, Gabriela Quaresma, and the Tenant attended the hearing.
The parties elected to participate in LTB facilitated mediation with the assistance of Anibal Benitez Calderon, a Dispute Resolution Officer and Hearing Officer with the Landlord and Tenant Board.
The parties agreed to resolve all the issues in the application and requested and order on consent. I am satisfied that the parties understood the terms of their consent as set out in the order below.
It is ordered on consent that:
1. The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord $6,484.86. This amount represents arrears owing to July 31, 2026 plus the Landlord’s cost of filing the application.
2. The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord the amount set out in paragraph 1 in accordance with the following schedule:
a) $1,050.00 on or before the 25th day of each month, commencing on August 25, 2026 up to and including December 25, 2026, and
4. If the Tenant fails to make any one of the payments in accordance with this order, the Landlord may, without notice to the Tenant, apply to the LTB within 30 days of the Tenant's breach pursuant to section 78 of the Act for an order terminating the tenancy and evicting the Tenant.
